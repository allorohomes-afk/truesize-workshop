The Workshop · how to use it in 5 minutes

Print this page (⌘P) for a one-page cheat sheet.

1 · Open it

  • Open the Workshop address on your iPad, phone or laptop.
  • Pick your workspace at the top. Your projects, palettes and look are kept there and follow you between devices.
  • Tap ? Guide any time to see this page.

2 · Start an event

  • New → pick a template: baby shower, birthday, bridal shower, wedding, graduation or gala. It's built at real sizes in your palette.
  • Or start blank and add pieces from the Add and Decor rows.
  • Reuse a layout: New → Save what's on screen as a template.

3 · Just say it

Type in the box, or hold 🎙 Hold to talk, speak, and let go. The assistant builds it and answers out loud.

  • "a 30 by 40 foot ballroom with 12 foot ceilings"
  • "a 9 foot organic arch by the back wall"
  • "two 60 inch round tables with flower centrepieces"
  • "make the arch 7 feet" · "remove the chairs"

4 · Your colours

  • Pick a palette at the top of the right panel. New pieces use it, and so does the assistant when you don't name colours.
  • Edit makes your own palette (up to 5 colours) and saves it in your workspace.
  • The look (Dark, Light, Blush & gold), units and voice are at the top.

5 · Measure and check

  • 📏 Measure, then tap two points: distance, rise and angle.
  • Tap any piece to see its exact size on the left.
  • 👁 Eye level shows what a guest sees; Fit view shows everything.
  • The Shopping list counts every balloon by size, colour and finish, plus tables, chairs and decor.

6 · Pull apart, change one balloon

  • Pull apart: the walls fold down and every piece separates, with labels.
  • Zoom in to read each balloon's size, colour and finish.
  • Tap one balloon to change its size, colour or finish, or every balloon like it.
  • Put together puts it back.

7 · Show the client

  • ✨ Render it turns the view into a photo-style picture in about a minute. Use Eye level first.
  • The render is an artist's impression; the 3D view and measurements are the real plan.
  • Snapshot saves the 3D view as a picture.

8 · Real rooms

  • On an iPad Pro or iPhone Pro, open the scan app, name the room, walk around it slowly, then Send to Workshop.
  • Here: 📐 Import scan → pick the room. Walls, doors and windows come in at their real size.
  • Scan the venues you use often once, and reuse them.

Good to know

  • Save often; Open lists your projects. Undo is in the Tools row.
  • Sizes are always calculated by the Workshop, not guessed by the AI, so "9 ft" is 9 ft and "two tables" is two.
  • Everything runs on the family's own computers. Nothing is sent to the internet, and there's no account or subscription.
